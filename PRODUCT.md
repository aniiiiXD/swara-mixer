# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Singers and music learners use Tonic to practise their voice, work with songs they enjoy, inspect pitch, and prepare backing tracks. They range from focused daily-practice users to creators who want practical stem controls and pitch analysis without navigating a full DAW.

## Product Purpose

Tonic turns a song and a voice into useful, understandable practice. Coach helps a singer improve; Mix makes a song's stems workable; Pitch makes vocal and instrumental pitch visible. The shared engine should make these tools feel like one product while letting each workflow stay focused.

## Brand Personality

Musical, clear, and quietly expert. Coach is warm and encouraging. Mix and Pitch are calm, precise workbenches that respect a user's concentration.

## Anti-references

Avoid a generic AI dashboard, a decorative consumer karaoke toy, and a crowded imitation DAW. Do not hide unrelated workflows behind mode switches, overwhelm users with controls before a track is ready, or use visual decoration in place of useful audio feedback.

## Design Principles

1. One intent per surface: practise, mix, or inspect.
2. Make audio state legible before exposing advanced control.
3. Keep the engine invisible and the music tangible.
4. Preserve continuity across tools without forcing one visual mood everywhere.
5. Prefer durable shared primitives over copied feature logic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. All core transport and mixing actions must be keyboard-operable with visible focus. Respect reduced-motion preferences, and do not encode pitch or error state by colour alone.
